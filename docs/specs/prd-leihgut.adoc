== Problem Statement

A "Bibliothek der Dinge" (library of things) lends tools and devices to its members.
Today the process is managed with a ring-binder and cash deposits.
Three concrete failures recur in production:

* Deposits are paid out in the wrong amount.
* Items due for maintenance are handed out despite being blocked.
* Reservations are ignored when a loan is extended.

The system replaces the ring-binder with a digitised lending process that enforces all three constraints automatically.
[`README.adoc:11-24`]

== Target Users

Three personas use the system:

[cols="1,2,3"]
|===
| Persona | Role identifier | Primary concern

| Karim
| `thekendienst` (counter staff)
| Instant authorisation decision at handout — can this item go out right now?

| Sabine
| `mitglied` (member)
| Check availability and place a reservation without visiting the counter.

| Rolf
| `wart` (workshop manager)
| Close inspections, finish maintenance, record training certifications, and manage the catalogue.
|===

A fourth technical actor (`admin`) manages audit-log retention.
[`README.adoc:43-49`, `src/leihgut/adapters/rest/rollen.py:13-27`, `src/leihgut/adapters/rest/app.py:541-561`]

== Goals

The system must ensure:

. The counter always knows — instantly — whether an item may be issued.
. Every deposit movement (collection, deduction on damage, seizure on loss) is traceable.
. Items due for maintenance are withheld automatically.
. Reservations are honoured when loans are extended.

[`README.adoc:61-80`]

== Channels and Interface

The system exposes one REST API (FastAPI) and a CLI.
There is deliberately no web UI in release 1.
[`README.adoc:50-59`, `src/leihgut/adapters/rest/app.py:254-592`]

== Scope

=== In scope

* Checkout, extension, and return of items (with deposit lifecycle).
* Inspection close with damage recording and deposit deduction.
* Loss declaration with 100 % deposit seizure and item decommission.
* Reservation (FIFO queue per category).
* Training certification and revocation.
* Catalogue management (categories and items).
* Availability query.
* Audit-log retention management.

=== Out of scope

* Member management — member identity is an opaque string; there is no member creation, authentication, or profile management. [`src/leihgut/adapters/persistence/schema.sql:61-65`, `src/leihgut/domain/ausleihe.py:25`]
* Payment processing — no integration with payment systems.
* Web UI.

=== Delivery priority

Delivery priority across user segments is deferred and must be resolved before release planning.

_Gap: The Product Owner has not yet specified whether counter checkout, member self-service, or workshop operations should be delivered first._

== Constraints

. Single locally startable process; no external services. [`README.adoc:50-59`]
. REST API and CLI only; no web UI. [`README.adoc:50-59`]
. No real authentication in release 1 — the role is read from an `X-Rolle` HTTP header and is not cryptographically verified. This is a deliberate, acknowledged constraint. [`README.adoc:57-58`, `src/leihgut/adapters/rest/rollen.py:1-27`]
. Date/time comes from a replaceable `Clock` port so that deadlines are fully testable. [`src/leihgut/ports/clock.py:1-18`]
